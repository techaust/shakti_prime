# Auditor 3: domain correctness (packages/domain and the database functions that hold domain rules)

Audited `main` at 23f78a5, read only. The pure engines were exercised with a throwaway script in the scratchpad (`scratchpad/dom3/check.ts`, run with the repo's `tsx`): tax lines and documents, rate boundaries at midnight IST, place of supply, quote validity, commission and credit outcomes. Live database checks were run as `app_user` inside `begin … rollback`: the `quotes` and `sales_orders` indexes, the foreign keys of `quotes`, the source of `app.merge_leads()`, the `accounts` column grants and triggers, and the `opportunities` and `quotes` read policies. The local database has the seed only (no users or customers), so the command-level failure paths below were confirmed by reading each code path end to end, not by running them.

## Score

**7.5 / 10.** The core is in good shape:
- The tax engine is pure, works in bigint paise and rounds half-up per head and per document, as ADR 0007 says. The script's cases came out exact.
- Rates are effective-dated on the IST calendar day, and the exclusion constraints back them.
- Prices come only from the approved list of the customer's tier (strict inputs, no price field, no discounts), and lines snapshot the rate or composite rule with its percentages.
- Every state change goes through `transition()`, which checks permission and guard, under a `FOR UPDATE` lock on the row.
- The gapless numbering, the advisory lock on dealer credit, the idempotency claim inside the command's transaction, and the audit and outbox writes in the same transaction are all correct.
- The definers that re-check the domain's arithmetic (`app.record_commission_accrual()`) agree with the TypeScript to the paisa.

The score is held back by gaps where one module's rule does not reach a neighbouring one:
- A customer's type, which any holder of `crm.account.write` can change, decides both whether the dealer credit check runs and whether an order may skip the quote. This is the same hole the tier guard closed for price tiers.
- Lead merge and import rollback archive a lead without looking at its quotes and orders, so its sale gets stranded.
- A lead can carry several accepted quotes, giving several orders and several commissions.

To reach 10:
- Fix DOM-1 to DOM-4.
- Snapshot the facts each money rule depends on (the account type, and the sized kW of the quote) onto the order, rather than re-reading values a person can still change.
- Add command tests that cross module lines: merge or roll back a lead that has a quote, and change a customer's type while one of their orders is a draft.

## Checked and sound

- **Tax engine** (`src/tax`). Integer paise, with quantity × price rounded half-up to the paisa. CGST and SGST are each half the rate, rounded separately; IGST uses the full rate. A composite 70:30 line puts the goods share half-up with services as the remainder, and rounds each head once per line. The document rounds to the rupee with the round-off between −0.49 and +0.50. Rates are effective on `[from, to)` by IST calendar day: the script showed 23:59 IST on 30-09 resolving to the old row and 00:00 IST on 01-10 to the new one. An item's own rate wins over its HSN rate. Place of supply goes site → GSTIN state → company, with the GSTIN regex enforced. A kit outside a works contract is refused. Amounts past `numeric(14,2)` are refused in plain words. Lines snapshot `tax_rate_id`, `tax_rate_pct`, the composite rule and its rates.
- **Prices** come only from the approved list of the customer's own tier (set only with `pricing.write:all`, enforced by `accounts_tier_guard`): the company's own list first, else the group's. The strict inputs of quotes and orders carry no price. `price_list_items.price >= 0`. Nothing named discount exists anywhere in contracts, domain, schema or web.
- **State machines.** `defineMachine` checks reachability, terminal states, unknown permissions and catalogue events at import. Every built machine's commands go through `transition()` with `authorise()` and the guards, and `machines.test.ts` ties `stored` to the schema. The DB-side `app.lapsed_quotes()` and `app.expire_quotes()` use the same `draft`/`sent` plus `valid_until < now()` rule as the TS `pastValidity` guard. Quote validity is the created IST date + 15 days, ending at 23:59:59.999 IST (checked in the script).
- **Commands.** All 126 `defineCommand`s are in the registry, which the test checks both ways. The runner refuses undeclared audit fields outside production, translates SQLSTATEs, and writes the audit and outbox rows, and the idempotency completion, in the command's transaction. The idempotency claim makes a concurrent repeat wait for the first to commit, and a rolled-back call leaves no key. `ctx.run` merges inner audit and events and records the outer stage only. `peopleOnly` is set on every sales, credit, targets, calls and merge command an agent must not run. Each command's permission and scope match the SECURITY §3.2 matrix (tabulated from all 126).
- **Concurrency.** Quotes, orders and leads are locked `FOR UPDATE` before a move. Dealer confirmations queue on an advisory lock per company and dealer, so the second sees the first as confirmed. The tax-rate and price changes lock the open row. Numbering uses `app.next_document_no()` with permission and company checks. `quotes_supersedes_unique`, `sales_orders_quote_unique`, `quotes_signed_file_unique` and `commission_accruals_order_unique` are mapped in `constraintReasons`.
- **Credit check.** Exposure (outstanding + confirmed unpaid since the entry + this order) is compared with `> limit`. A missing limit fails closed. Overdue is the age in whole IST days of the oldest unpaid invoice compared with `> credit_days`. A hold is committed, not refused, and notified once per rule. Only the Executive's release in their own name passes, and the trigger enforces it. Customer merges move a dealer's terms and outstanding only when the kept customer has none, so a debt is never counted twice.
- **Commission.** The rule in force on the IST confirmation date is chosen partner first, then the group default, with no overlaps (exclusion constraint). TS `commissionAmount()` and the definer agree for `fixed`, `percent` and per-kW/per-HP (checked to the paisa; module kWp always has three decimals, since `wp` is a whole number). The accrual is cancelled together with a confirmed order.
- **Targets.** Period starts and bounds are in IST (Monday weeks, first-of-month), and a value of 0 removes a target. Actuals are counted only in `app.target_actuals()`, which refuses agents and the system principal.
- **Sizing.** Hazen-Williams TDH, pump power with the motor margin and the next standard HP, the solar array and the rooftop limits are pure functions with whole-module epsilon rounding and property tests. The DCR and sanctioned-load rules feed the quote's `create` guard. Only a person's current sizing counts (`latestSizing`).
- **Telecom.** `checkDial()` gives 140 for promotional (refused on DND) and 160 for service (needs consent); TRAI hours are 09:00 to before 21:00 IST. Retry and nurture schedules start at the beginning of IST calling hours. Consent withdrawal blocks both the number reveal and logging a call. DND scrubbing and the dialer are Phase 2 by design (§1 of the Phase 1 design).
- **Money and time in TS.** No float arithmetic on rupees in domain code. Displays go through `formatRupees` and `formatDate` in IST. IST day boundaries are used in quotes, credit, the queue's "today", home and targets.
- **Dispatch machine** (Phase 3, data only): the e-way gate requires a bill number, a validity and a vehicle when the value is above ₹50,000.
