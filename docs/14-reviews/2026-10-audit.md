## 1. Summary
No critical finding. Three high findings, each with a real path:
- **DOM-1:** anyone who may edit a customer can change its type, which lets a dealer order confirm without the credit check, or a household order skip the quote, sizing and signed acceptance; reproduced as a Lead Converter confirming ₹6,72,000 with no Executive release.
- **SEC-1:** an import keeps every column of the client's file, an Aadhaar column included, unmasked in `import_rows.raw_json` and the stored file, with no purge (readable by the Executive and the GM).
- **DATA-1:** "Send again" on an event whose worker failed is dropped by QStash's deduplication and still marked delivered.
The web app, tests, CI and architecture have no high finding.

The hardest parts hold: forced, fail-closed row-level security on every table; the cost gate; the tax engine to the paisa, the 70:30 split and the IST day boundary; prices only from the Price Master; the command runner's audit, outbox and idempotency in one transaction; 77 of 77 tables in the testing lists; every GitHub Action pinned with minimal permissions.

| Step | What was done |

The data-layer findings' verification and the hot reads measured under RLS with statistics were cut short by the plan's usage limit on 12-10-2026 and continue in the fix plan. |
