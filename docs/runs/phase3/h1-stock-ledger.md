## Brief
Build [phase3 §5](../../03-roadmap-appendix/phase3.md#5-the-stock-model) and [§6.2](../../03-roadmap-appendix/phase3.md#62-h1-hubs-bins-and-the-stock-ledger) as written. Follow [AGENTS §6](../../../AGENTS.md#6-database-changes) for each table and [AGENTS §5](../../../AGENTS.md#5-domain-commands) for each command. Points the design leaves to you, settled here:

1. **Tables:** `warehouses`, `bins`, `stock_movements`, `stock_movement_costs`, `stock_balances`, `serials` with the columns of §5; quantities `numeric(12,3)`, unit costs `numeric(14,4)`, money `numeric(14,2)`; uuidv7 keys (ADR 0006). `stock_movements` is not partitioned; index it for the stock card `(entity_id, item_id, moved_at desc)`, by bin, by serial and by `(ref_type, ref_id)`. Hubs and bins carry no company (shared, question 1 (a)); every other table carries `entity_id` and forced row security that fails closed.
2. **Row security and grants:** hubs and bins are read by any principal holding `inventory.stock.read` in some company and written only through the hub and bin commands with `inventory.stock.adjust` at `all` scope; movements, balances and serials are read by company with `inventory.stock.read` (a Field Engineer's `own` scope reads nothing until Phase 4 says what own means for stock: no rows); `stock_movement_costs` and `item_costs` only with `finance.cost.read` (reuse the `cost_gate` pattern of migration 0008). `app_user` and `app_reader` get no insert, update or delete on `stock_movements`, `stock_movement_costs`, `stock_balances` or `item_costs`: only the definers below write them. Add each table to the testing lists, the role × company fixture and `app_reader`'s grants as AGENTS §6 says, and `NARROWER` where it applies.
3. **`app.stock_move(movements jsonb)`**, security definer, the one write path (§5): takes a batch (each: company, item, serial or null, from bin or null, to bin or null, quantity, reason, reference, reverses or null, unit cost or null); checks the caller's grant per reason inside the function from the request's settings (`opening`, `adjust_gain`, `adjust_loss`, `damage`, `reversal` and `count` need `inventory.stock.adjust`; `transfer` needs `inventory.stock.move`; the other reasons are refused for now with `stock_reason_not_open`, each later slice opening its own reason with its own grant); refuses a company outside `app.entity_ids`; locks the balance rows it touches in one fixed order (company, hub, bin, item, by id) with `select … for update` after inserting missing rows; refuses a bin of another hub's transit pair, an archived bin, an inactive or unstocked item (`items.is_stocked` arrives with C5: read it if present, else treat every item as stocked, and say so in the report), a serial on an untracked item or none on a tracked one, a quantity other than 1 for a serial, a serial not in the from bin, and any bin going below zero (`stock_insufficient`, naming the item and bin); writes the movements, their costs (inbound: the given unit cost, else the moving average; outbound: the moving average), the balances, the serial's state and bin, and the moving average in `item_costs` (inbound with a cost revalues; the STOCK-2 rule of question 2 (a): an inbound movement while the company's on-hand is at or below zero takes the last positive average, kept in `item_costs`); answers the movement ids and never a cost. `app.stock_balance_check()` (for G3's nightly alert and your property test) answers the rows where a balance differs from its movements, for the workers principal and tests only.
4. **Serials:** the `serial` machine in `packages/domain/src/state-machines/machines/` with the eight states of §5; in H1 only `opening` and `adjust_gain` create serials (`in_stock`), `adjust_loss`, `damage` and `count` losses end them (`scrapped`), `transfer` moves them; the other transitions are specified for later slices and marked so. `serial_no` is unique per item (case-insensitive, trimmed). Expose `existingSerialsOf(itemId)` for C5's pattern check and say in your report how it is to be wired after the merge.
5. **Commands** (`packages/domain/src/commands/inventory/`): `inventory.hub.create`, `.update`, `.archive`; `inventory.bin.create`, `.update`, `.archive` (refused while it holds stock); `inventory.stock.transfer`; `inventory.stock.adjust`; `inventory.stock.reverse` (within `WORKSHOP_DEFAULTS.stock.reversalDays`, 7, never a reversal of a reversal); each through `defineCommand` with `auditFields` that never include a cost, idempotency keys, the denied, wrong-company and happy-path tests, and the event `inventory.stock.moved` (ids, items, bins, quantities, reasons; no cost).
6. **Opening stock:** an import kind `opening_stock` (company, hub code, bin code, SKU, quantity, unit cost, serial) posting one batch per import through `app.stock_move()` with reason `opening`; a company that has an opening batch not wholly reversed is refused (`opening_stock_posted`). Every hub gets its `receiving` and `dispatch` bins when created.
7. **Queries and screens:** `listStockBalances` (keyset, by hub, bin, item, category, with free and reserved), `getStockCard` (an item's movements by company), `listHubs`, `getSerial`, `readStockValuation` (`finance.cost.read`; quantity, average cost and value per company, hub and category at a date, the value from the movements' costs up to that date).
