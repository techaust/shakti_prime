**Decisions the brief did not settle:**
1. An inbound IVR call has no lead, so `provider_calls.opportunity_id` became nullable (the dial's meaning is unchanged and checked); such a row is read by nobody under `provider_calls_read`. It names no phone (the number is never copied to a call); a missed call names the customer's phone on its lead.
2. An IVR call is recorded `ringing`; with no status report for inbound calls in T4, T3's hourly sweep closes it as `failed` after three hours, as any call whose end never came. A missed call is recorded `no_answer`, ended at its start.
3. A missed call's lead starts in the pipeline an unknown number's WhatsApp message does (`WORKSHOP_DEFAULTS.whatsapp.leadPipelineKey`, `farmer_pumps`, already flagged for the sales head), not a new named default; a company with no such pipeline sets the delivery aside as `lead_pipeline_missing`.
4. A missed call with no owner on its lead (nobody present, no Sales Team Lead) gets no callback; an open callback of the owner's already due by then is kept rather than a second made.
5. A number shared by several companies' lines records nothing (`call_number_shared`); two people with one dial phone ring nobody.
6. The ringing call waits in the key-value store a minute (`RINGING_TTL_SECONDS`), the later call replacing an earlier one; it holds the caller's number for the new-lead form, and `ScreenPopDto` gives it out only when no customer of the company holds it (otherwise the last four digits).
7. The callback's hours are checked again in the definer (09:00 to 21:00 IST), not that it is in the future, so the suites can set the clock.
