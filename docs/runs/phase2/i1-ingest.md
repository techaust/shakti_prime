**Decisions the brief did not settle:**
1. **The table is `ingest_sources`, not `lead_sources`:** `lead_sources` is already the group's list of channels (0004, shared, `opportunities.source_id` names it). A lead from an integration keeps `source_id` = its channel's row (`meta_ads`, `google_ads`, `website`, `whatsapp`…); `ingest_sources` is one company's connection of a form, Page or site. The DTOs are `IngestSource*` because `LeadSourceDto` exists; the commands keep the design's names (`crm.source.*`).
2. `ingest_sources` beyond §6.2: `name`; `consent_channels` and `consent_purpose` (a consent row needs what the tick consents to: set by the Executive with the question, never guessed); `last_questions_json` and `last_lead_at` (the field map's preview); `page_token_set`; `archived_at` in place of `is_active`; no `segment` (the pipeline gives it). `ingest_keys` adds `key_hint`, `last_used_at`, `revoked_by`, `created_by`.
3. The website key is made by the caller (`newIngestKey()`), shown once by the action; the command receives only its hash and last four characters, so the key never reaches an audit row, a stored idempotent answer or a log.
4. Because the platform may not write leads, customers or the timeline under their policies, the writes are one definer (`app.ingest_lead_write()`); what is shared with `crm.lead.create` in TypeScript is the number lock, the `crm.lead.created` event, the picker and the scoring (`scoreLead()` through `app.write_lead_scores()`, hence `alsoRequires: crm.score.refresh`, which `system:workers` holds).
5. Dedupe for the platform: a number held by any customer of the company is that customer, whatever the name (for a person a colleague's customer is refused and routed, AUDIT M25; the platform has no "own" customers). The new lead goes to the colleague who looks after them (`customer_owner`) with routed work, if they still work on leads there; a repeat enquiry for the same business line joins the open lead (activity in 30 days) or the lead in nurture, which is not reopened (reopening is a person's `crm.lead.write`), and fills only the facts it lacks. Duplicate cards are left to the nightly search, as for an import.
6. Question 3: the candidates are the company's active `tele_caller_cc` people holding `crm.lead.write`, present by their caller profile, under its cap, of its languages and business lines (the converter switch is not read); fewest open leads, ties in turn after the one the platform gave a lead to last (read from the leads, no Redis cursor); one intake at a time per company (an advisory lock). Else the company's first Sales Team Lead by id, with routed work and `crm.enquiry.routed`; else the lead is unassigned (`assignedTo: unassigned`), seen by whoever reads the company's leads, rather than lost to retries.
7. A new customer's type by business line: farm (pumps), household (rooftop), business (commercial, and dealer enquiries: only an Executive makes a dealer, DOM-1). A site only when a village or PIN came, typed borewell, rooftop or factory by business line; none for a dealer enquiry.
8. A form consent is recorded with source `web_form`, one row per channel, given when the person ticked it (never in the future).
9. The form's free-text message is a `note` on the lead's timeline written by the platform; the qualification set writes no timeline row (no new activity type; it is audited).
10. `discom_consumer_no` is a qualification fact but not a stage exit field (only the five measures are in `StageExitFieldSchema`); `monthly_bill_rupees` is `numeric(14,2)`, whole rupees in the contract.
11. Audit field names `leadChannel`, `ingestOutcome`, `sourceProvider` (not `channel`, `outcome`, `provider`, which already read through other value lists).
