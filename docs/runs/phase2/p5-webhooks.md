### Fix round, 13-11-2026, cloud session (Postgres 54322)

**What changed:**
- Process again counts ten tries afresh (`attempts_at_replay`), `attempts` keeps rising, and the replayed row is claimable at once (`retry_at` now); the claim fails at `attempts - attempts_at_replay >= 10` and says so, and the worker reports it with an `error` line and answers `failed` (findings 1, 9).
- A handler error releases the row into a backoff of 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 30 minutes and answers `retrying` (a new `WebhookProcessResult` outcome), so QStash's chain ends; the sweep republishes only rows no delivery is on its way to, a minute after their arrival, lapsed lease, backoff or replay. Ten tries now span about two and a half hours (finding 2). `handler_failed` remains the failure callback's code, for a worker that never answered.
- The 90-day removal takes processed rows and failed rows no live lease holds, never a waiting or leased one; a purge writes a `retention_runs` row only when it removed something or failed, and stays in the minute sweep (findings 3, 8).
- Sentry: `withoutQuery()` also replaces `/webhooks/exotel/incoming/<segment>` with `[secret]`, which covers the request, the transaction name, span address fields and breadcrumbs (finding 4).
- Google's `google_key` is not stored; the worker reads the stored lead with `StoredGoogleLead` (finding 5).
- Every check returns `no_secret` for an empty secret; `productionConfigProblems()` refuses a `GOOGLE_LEADFORM_KEY` or `EXOTEL_INCOMING_SECRET` under 32 characters (finding 6).
- The sweep publishes in one QStash batch call (`publishWebhookJobs`) given what is left of its 20-second budget, at most 10 seconds (finding 7).
- Wording: "the ten of 0142"; decision 5 states the attempt rule as the code applies it (finding 10). The design's Built record gains decisions 16 to 21; DATABASE §4.1, §6.10 and API §3.6 follow the new behaviour.
