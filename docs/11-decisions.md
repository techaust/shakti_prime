## Standing rules in force
The decisions that govern day-to-day work now, each with its row below:
- **Where work runs:** each session wholly on the PC or wholly in a cloud session, as the owner chooses that day (09-10-2026). On the PC: Docker Desktop, at most two slice builders, heavy commands one at a time through `bash tools/integration/heavy.sh <command>`. In the cloud: [hybrid](runbooks/hybrid.md), up to three builders in the lead's VM, pull requests and the hosted steps from the cloud session.
- **Models and usage, final for the whole project:** only Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5, Fable 5.1 not used; effort by one rule (medium to carry out, high for judgement, xhigh where a miss reaches production; max and low not used); Tier A built by Opus 5.5 at high and reviewed at xhigh, Tier B built at medium and reviewed at high, Tier C built by Sonnet 5.5 at high and reviewed by Opus 5.5 at medium; every phase's slices tiered by the same table; Haiku 5.5 for searches (`Explore`), suites and document drafts; the lead on Opus 5.5 at medium every day, at xhigh for a named task; a budget gate on the 5-hour window before every agent, posted by the owner in the cloud: new agents at or under 80 %, nothing new at 90 % or more, and no weekly limit on the Team plan's Premium seat (26-10-2026); no model or effort changes to save usage, the gate sets the pace; the lead reports on events only ([models-and-usage](runbooks/models-and-usage.md); 12-10-2026).
- **Session phrases:** the owner starts each day with "start the day" and ends it with "end the day" (07-10-2026).
- **Merging:** every pull request merges itself when CI passes; nobody merges by hand or pushes to `main` (27-09-2026).
- **Hosted environments:** the standing go-ahead covers the migration, redeploy and health checks of dev and staging, and their QStash schedules; everything else asks the owner first (29-09-2026, 05-10-2026).
- **Product:** English on every screen; prices only from Price Master tiers, with no discounts; agents are never Automatic in Phase 1 (27-09-2026, 26-09-2026, 05-10-2026).
- **Repository:** public while development continues; made private by the owner afterwards, with GitHub Pro from that moment (06-10-2026, 15-10-2026).
- **Production:** at `https://shaktiprime.com`, deployed only by a release the owner approves (15-10-2026).
