## 1. Scope
**What Phases 1 and 2 left for this phase:** the catalogue (`items` with SKU, category specifications, HSN, unit, serial tracking, DCR flag and ALMM reference; one-level `kits`; `pump_curves`), the Price Master and the tax tables are built (C1). Sales orders exist in draft, confirmed and cancelled, and their machine already holds `dispatch.partial` and `dispatch.complete` with no command behind them (S2). `item_costs` exists with its cost policy and no writer. The permissions `inventory.*`, `procurement.*` and the two cost grants are in the catalogue and the seed, checked by no command. The `dispatch` and `warranty-claim` machines are specified and not in use. The number series accept `challan` and `purchase_order` (codes `DC` and `PO`). A QR label template at 50 × 25 mm and 100 × 50 mm exists for the print spike (`apps/web/src/print/label-template.ts`), outside the document registry. No warehouse, bin, ledger, serial, reservation, vendor, purchase order, receipt, dispatch, claim or surplus table exists.

**In Phase 3:**
- the full catalogue for stock: warranty terms, supplier barcodes, purchase units, a serial pattern per item, and the item import;
- hubs and bins at both sites, the append-only stock ledger with reason codes, balances per company, hub, bin and item, moving-average cost on the restricted side, serials and their states, adjustments, transfers, the opening stock import and the stock valuation report;
- kits' availability-to-promise as a pure, tested function; reservations against order lines on confirmation, backorders, expiry and release; availability on quote and order lines;
- the vendor master, supplier quotes and the comparison matrix behind `procurement.rate.read`; reorder levels and alerts that draft purchase orders; purchase order approval and its PDF; goods receipt with every serial captured by handheld scanner or camera;
- QR labels for serials, bins and packages from the shared print templates;
- dispatch: split dispatch from several hubs, picking from reservations by scan, the delivery challan, vehicle and driver, the e-way bill gate, in transit, "materials arrived" with a photo, the order's dispatch states, the `dispatched` WhatsApp milestone, and transfers between hubs;
- returns after dispatch and the zero-value surplus pool;
- warranty claims and supplier returns through the five steps of BLUEPRINT §8.4, with an installed-base import for serials sold before go-live;
- stock counts: blind counts per hub and bin, the variance review and its posting, the tool the exit gate's physical audit uses;
- the Inventory home, stock reports and serials in ⌘K search.

**In later phases (BLUEPRINT §14):** DCR/ALMM serial validation against a project's subsidy requirements, the field engineer recording arrival and leftovers in the Android app, and the surplus pool's project link (Phase 4); `job_cost_entries`, `tally_purchase_vouchers` and the monthly reconciliation of the BOS stock valuation with Tally's closing stock (Phase 5); the Sizing and Quote agent reading stock (Phase 6). **In the final phase (the owner's decision of 13-11-2026):** every step that needs the client's people: the hubs' bin layout, the opening stock from the client's count, the vendor list and rates, reorder levels, the label printer and stock, the scanners, the e-way bill practice (STOCK-3), and the physical stock audit at both sites. ## 2. Decisions for the owner
The owner decided all eleven on 23-11-2026, each the recommended option (a), as marked below and recorded in [DECISIONS](../11-decisions.md).

1. **Who owns stock** (every slice; BLUEPRINT §6.1 makes the companies selling entities that share staff): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* stock belongs to a company. Every movement, balance, serial, reservation, purchase order, receipt and dispatch carries one company, and row security reads it as it reads orders. Hubs and bins are shared by the group, so both sites hold several companies' stock side by side. Goods pass from one company to another only by a sale between them (an order and a dispatch with its challan and invoice), never by a ledger transfer, since each company's stock is valued in its own Tally company (BLUEPRINT §8.8) and GST treats the move as a supply.
   - (b) One group pool, with the company named only on documents.
   - (c) Stock per company, with a free internal transfer between companies.
2. **Stock cost** (H1, U2; workshop STOCK-1 and STOCK-2 for the CA): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* a moving average per company and item. A receipt revalues the average; an issue takes the average at that moment. Each movement's cost is kept on the restricted side (`stock_movement_costs`, `finance.cost.read`) and the average in `item_costs`. A return while stock stands at or below zero takes the last positive average (STOCK-2's third option) as a named default until the CA decides.
   - (b) A moving average that may go negative, corrected at the next purchase.
   - (c) First in, first out cost layers.
3. **When stock is set aside for an order, and for how long** (H2; workshop STOCK-4): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* confirming an order reserves free stock for each line at once (a kit line reserves its components); what is short becomes a backorder, filled in order of confirmation as stock arrives. A reservation holds 15 days (named default); the order's owner and the Inventory Manager are told 2 days before it lapses, and the Inventory Manager or the General Manager may extend it once by the same period. The STOCK-4 answer replaces both numbers.
   - (b) Held until the order is dispatched or cancelled, with no expiry.
   - (c) Reserved by hand by the Inventory Manager, never on confirmation.
4. **What a Lead Converter sees of stock on a quote** (H2; PRD SAL-04 asks for availability on quotes, SECURITY §3.2 gives tele-callers no `inventory.stock.read`): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* each quote and order line shows a status, not a count: in stock, partly in stock, or on order with the expected date, answered by a definer for holders of `sales.quote.create`; the Inventory Manager, the General Manager and the Executive see the quantities.
   - (b) Lead Converters get `inventory.stock.read` for their company and see the quantities.
   - (c) No availability on quotes; orders only.
5. **Who approves a purchase order** (U2; BLUEPRINT §8.4: "a reorder alert drafts a PO that a person approves"): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* a new `procurement.po.approve`. The Inventory Manager approves a purchase order below ₹2,00,000 for their company, the Executive any amount (the limit a named default, `WORKSHOP_DEFAULTS.procurement.poApprovalFromRupees`). Nobody approves a purchase order they drafted, unless they are an Executive.
   - (b) The Executive approves every purchase order.
   - (c) The Inventory Manager approves every purchase order.
6. **Vendors shared or per company** (U1): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* one vendor master for the group, as customers are shared (ADR 0008); a supplier quote may name the company it was given to; a purchase order belongs to one company. Any holder of `procurement.po.write` adds or edits a vendor, audited; rates stay behind `procurement.rate.read`.
   - (b) A vendor list per company.
7. **Who records "materials arrived" in Phase 3** (D2; the dispatch machine's note: field engineers hold no dispatch permission and the field app is Phase 4): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* the holders of `inventory.dispatch.write` (the Inventory Manager, the Project Manager, the General Manager), from any browser with the photo taken on the phone; Phase 4 adds `inventory.dispatch.arrive` for a field engineer on their own assigned jobs in the app.
   - (b) Arrival is recorded without a photo until the field app exists.
8. **Scanning serials and labels** (U2, D2, H3): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* handheld scanners, which type into the focused field, and the camera through the browser: the browser's own barcode reader where it has one (Chrome on Android reads QR and the usual one-dimensional codes), else the installed `jsqr` for QR codes only; no new package. A one-dimensional supplier barcode on a laptop without a reader is typed or scanned with a handheld.
   - (b) Add a barcode-reading package (`@zxing/browser`) so every browser's camera reads one-dimensional codes; a new dependency needs the owner's go-ahead.
9. **Labels** (Q1; PRD INV-07): **Decided: (a).**
   - (a) *Recommended:* PDF labels rendered by the existing Chromium print path at the label stock's exact size (50 × 25 mm for serials and bins, 100 × 50 mm for packages), with QR codes from the installed `qrcode`, printed from the browser to whichever label printer the hub has; the printer and stock the client names are checked in the final phase (INV-07's criterion).
   - (b) The printer's own language (ZPL for Zebra printers), which needs the model chosen now.
10. **The cost of a warranty replacement before job costing exists** (R3; the `warranty-claim` machine's `cost_entry` writes a `job_cost_entries` row, a Phase 5 table): **Decided: (a).**
    - (a) *Recommended:* the replacement's issue carries its moving-average cost on the restricted side of its movement, and the claim keeps the cost and any supplier credit in restricted columns (`finance.cost.read`); Phase 5 turns each into a job cost entry against the project or order.
    - (b) Claims carry no cost until Phase 5.
11. **The surplus pool before projects exist** (R2; `surplus_pool.project_id` names a Phase 4 table): **Decided: (a).**
    - (a) *Recommended:* the Inventory Manager logs leftovers returned from a delivered dispatch or an order into a surplus bin at zero value; they count apart from free stock, and are suggested when a later reservation or pick needs the same item. Phase 4 adds the project link and logging from the field app.
    - (b) The surplus pool waits for Phase 4.

## 5. The stock model
Settled here for every slice, subject to questions 1 to 3:
- **Hubs** (`warehouses`): shared by the group, each with a code, name, kind (`godown`, `factory`, `transit`), address, state code and PIN code. **Bins** belong to a hub, each with a code unique in its hub and a kind: `storage`, `receiving`, `dispatch`, `quarantine` (faulty and returned goods awaiting a decision) or `surplus`. Every hub has one receiving and one dispatch bin; the transit hub has one bin per hub pair.
- **The ledger** (`stock_movements`, append-only): `entity_id`, `item_id`, `serial_id` (null for untracked items), `from_bin_id`, `to_bin_id` (one of them null for a receipt or an issue), `qty` (positive, `numeric(12,3)` in the stock unit), `reason`, `ref_type` and `ref_id` (the document that caused it), `reverses_id`, `moved_at`, `moved_by`, `request_id`. Reasons: `opening`, `receipt`, `transfer`, `pick`, `issue`, `return_customer`, `return_supplier`, `adjust_gain`, `adjust_loss`, `damage`, `count`, `warranty_replacement`, `surplus_in`, `surplus_out`, `reversal`. A movement is never updated or deleted; a correction is a reversal plus a new movement.
- **Balances** (`stock_balances`): one row per company, bin and item, with `qty_on_hand` and `qty_reserved`, maintained in the same transaction by the ledger's own definer, never written by a command; a test proves every balance equals the sum of its movements (INV-02). An issue or a transfer that would take a bin below zero is refused (`stock_insufficient`); free stock is on hand minus reserved in `storage` bins.
- **Costs** (`stock_movement_costs`, restricted, append-only): each movement's unit cost; `item_costs.moving_avg_cost` and `last_purchase_rate` per company and item, kept by the receipt and return definers. A count or adjustment gain enters at the moving average; surplus at zero.
- **Serials** (`serials`): `entity_id`, `item_id`, `serial_no` (unique per item), `state` (`in_stock`, `reserved`, `picked`, `in_transit`, `with_customer`, `faulty`, `with_supplier`, `scrapped`), `bin_id`, `account_id`, `site_id`, `vendor_id`, `receipt_line_id`, `delivered_at`, `warranty_until`, `supplier_warranty_until`, `dcr_validated_at` (Phase 4). A serial-tracked item moves one serial per movement; the ledger definer keeps the serial's state and bin in step with its movements. A new machine `serial` documents the states.
- **One write path:** every stock change goes through `app.stock_move()`, a definer that locks the balance rows it touches in a fixed order (company, hub, bin, item), checks the bin, the serial's state and the quantity, writes the movement, its cost and the balances, and answers the movement ids. Commands call it inside their transaction; nothing else writes the three tables.
- **Reads:** lists page by keyset with `EXPLAIN` evidence at 20,000 items, 200 bins and two million movements; the item's stock card reads its movements by `(entity_id, item_id, moved_at)`.

## 6. Wave 1

### 6.1 C5 Catalogue for stock
- **Columns on `items`:** `warranty_months` (to the customer, from delivery), `supplier_warranty_months` (from receipt), `barcode` (the supplier's code, unique where set), `purchase_unit` and `purchase_unit_factor` (a coil of 100 metres bought, metres stocked; factor ≥ 1, the stock unit stays `unit`), `serial_pattern` (an optional regular expression a scanned serial must match, to catch a scan of the wrong code), `is_stocked` (false for services and labour, which never reach the ledger). Specifications for the categories stock needs (structure, cable, pipe, inverter, battery) where C1's are thin.
- **Commands:** `catalogue.item.create` and `.update` take the new fields (All companies only, as now); a serial pattern that does not compile, or that the item's existing serials fail, is refused.
- **Import:** items through the imports framework (P2b) as a new import kind: SKU, name, category, HSN, unit, purchase unit and factor, serial tracking, DCR and ALMM, warranty months, barcode; a row with an unknown category or an HSN not of 4, 6 or 8 digits is refused with its reason; a repeated SKU updates the item.
- **Screens:** the item sheet in `/catalogue` gains Stock and warranty; the import is offered beside the items grid.
- **Acceptance:** INV-01's criteria hold for every new field; an import of 5,000 items finishes inside the import budget; a serial pattern is enforced at receipt (U2) and at count (H3).

**Built (C5):**
- `items` gains `warranty_months`, `supplier_warranty_months`, `barcode` (`items_barcode_unique`), `purchase_unit`, `purchase_unit_factor`, `serial_pattern` and `is_stocked`, each rule a check in the database (`items_warranty_months_check`, `_barcode_check`, `_purchase_unit_check`, `_purchase_factor_check`, `_serial_pattern_check`, `_stocked_check`) and the same in `CreateItemInput` and `UpdateItemInput`; only an Other item without serials is left out of stock (`NON_STOCK_CATEGORIES`).
- The serial pattern is compiled with the `u` flag and anchored (`compileSerialPattern()`, `serialPatternProblem()` in `@shakti/contracts`), refused when it does not compile, matches an empty serial, is longer than 200 characters or repeats a repeating group; `serialMatchesPattern()` and `serialsOutsidePattern()` in `packages/domain/src/catalogue/` serve U2 and H3; `catalogue.item.update` calls `serialsOutsidePattern()` with the item's serials once H1's `serials` exists.
- Optional specifications for structures (`capacityKwp`, `material`), cables (`cores`, `sizeMm2`, `conductor`), pipes (`pipeClass`, `pieceLengthM`), inverters (`kva`, `phase`) and batteries (`ah`, `voltageV`).
- `catalogue.item.create` and `.update` take and audit the terms; a barcode another item has answers `item_barcode_taken`; `barcode` is a long number by design (`unmaskedFields`).
- The import kind `items` (a group kind beside `pin_codes`): every row read and checked as the command input it becomes, a category, HSN code, unit, serial pattern or barcode refused with its reason, a category with required specifications sent to the item sheet (`item_details_needed`), a code repeated in the file skipped; each row committed one by one in its own savepoint through `catalogue.item.create` or `.update` as the importer, a row the catalogue no longer takes set aside with its reason; the rollback archives the items the file added, except one a kit still sold holds, and keeps its changes. 5,000 items on the local stack: the job made in 1.8 s, checked in 0.5 s, added in 37.1 s (12 batches), rolled back in 0.5 s.
- Screens: Stock and warranty on the item sheet and in the item form; Import items beside the items grid (`/imports/new?kind=items`).

### 6.2 H1 Hubs, bins and the stock ledger
- **Tables:** `warehouses`, `bins`, `stock_movements`, `stock_movement_costs`, `stock_balances`, `serials` as §5 gives them; `item_costs` gets its first writer. Row security: hubs and bins are read by every staff role holding `inventory.stock.read` and written with `inventory.stock.adjust` at all scope; movements, balances and serials by company with `inventory.stock.read`; costs with `finance.cost.read`.
- **Commands:** `inventory.hub.create`, `.update`, `.archive`, `inventory.bin.create`, `.update`, `.archive` (a bin holding stock cannot be archived); `inventory.stock.transfer` (bin to bin in one hub, `inventory.stock.move`); `inventory.stock.adjust` (gain, loss or damage with a reason of at most 300 characters and, for a serial-tracked item, the serials; `inventory.stock.adjust`); `inventory.stock.reverse` (a movement made in error, within 7 days, never a receipt, an issue or an opening line, which their own documents reverse; `inventory.stock.adjust`). Each audited, each emitting `inventory.stock.moved` with ids and quantities, never a cost.
- **Opening stock:** an import kind per company: hub, bin, SKU, quantity, unit cost, serials (one row per serial for tracked items); it posts `opening` movements in one batch per import and refuses a company that already has an opening batch posted, unless the earlier one is reversed whole.
- **The state machine** `serial` added to the registry and documented by `machines:docs`.
- **Screens:** `/inventory/stock` (menu Inventory › Stock): balances by hub, bin and item with free and reserved quantities, filters and saved views; the item's stock card (movements newest first, by hub); Adjust and Move between bins; `/inventory/hubs` for hubs and bins; `/inventory/serials/[id]` a serial's history. The valuation report (quantity, average cost, value per company, hub and category, at a date) at `/inventory/valuation` for `finance.cost.read` only.
- **Acceptance:** balances equal the sum of movements after 10,000 random movements (a property test); two concurrent issues of the last unit leave one refused; no screen, DTO, event or audit row of a person without `finance.cost.read` holds a cost; a serial is never in two bins.

## 7. Wave 2

### 7.1 H2 Reservations and availability
- **Availability-to-promise** (`packages/domain/src/stock/atp.ts`, pure): for an item, free stock in a company's storage bins, optionally per hub; for a kit, the largest number of kits the components' free stock makes (the floor of each component's free stock over its quantity per kit), with the limiting component named; for a line, whether it is covered, partly covered or short, and when receipts on open purchase orders would cover it. Tested on generated cases.
- **Table `reservations`:** `entity_id`, `sales_order_line_id`, `item_id` (a kit line reserves each component), `warehouse_id`, `qty`, `state` (`active`, `picked`, `released`, `lapsed`), `expires_at`, `extended_by`, `extended_at`, `released_reason`; a new machine `reservation`. `stock_balances.qty_reserved` is kept by `app.stock_reserve()` and `app.stock_unreserve()` beside `app.stock_move()`.
- **On confirmation** (question 3 (a)): `sales.order.confirm` reserves each line's free stock, hubs in the order's preferred order (the hub nearest the site's PIN code first, by a named list per hub, else the default hub); the short quantity is a backorder (`sales_order_lines.qty_backordered`). The order's cancellation releases its reservations (the machine's `release_reservations`).
- **The worker:** `inventory.stock.moved` with stock entering a storage bin runs `inventory.reservation.fill` (platform-only): backorders of that item and company in order of confirmation take the new free stock. A daily scan lapses reservations past `expires_at` (their stock becomes free, and the backorder returns) and tells the order's owner and the Inventory Manager 2 days before.
- **Commands:** `inventory.reservation.extend` (once, a reason; `inventory.stock.adjust`, or `sales.order.cancel` for the General Manager), `.release` (by hand, a reason), `.move` (to another hub's free stock). Each audited.
- **Availability on documents** (question 4 (a)): `app.line_availability(order or quote)` answers a status per line and, with `inventory.stock.read`, the quantities; the quote builder and the order page show it; a quote never reserves.
- **Acceptance:** INV-03's criteria; two orders confirmed at once for the last units reserve without overselling; a lapsed reservation frees its stock and the next backorder takes it; the order page shows reserved, backordered and dispatched quantities per line.

### 7.2 U2 Purchase orders and goods receipt
- **Tables:** `reorder_rules` (`entity_id`, `item_id`, `warehouse_id`, `reorder_level`, `reorder_qty`, in the stock unit); `purchase_orders` (`entity_id`, `po_no` from the `purchase_order` series, `vendor_id`, `deliver_to_warehouse_id`, `state`, `expected_at`, `drafted_by`, `approved_by`, `approved_at`, `sent_at`, `cancel_reason`, the vendor's state code and the place of supply for the tax split); `po_lines` (`item_id`, `qty` in purchase units, `qty_received`, `unit_rate` and `amount` restricted, `hsn`, `tax_rate_pct`); `goods_receipts` (`entity_id`, `grn_no`, `po_id`, `warehouse_id`, `received_at`, `received_by`, `vendor_invoice_no`, `vendor_invoice_date`); `goods_receipt_lines` (`po_line_id`, `item_id`, `bin_id` (receiving by default), `qty`, `unit_rate` restricted). A new series kind `goods_receipt` (code `GRN`). New machine `purchase-order`: `draft` → `approved` → `sent` → `partially_received` → `received` → `closed`, with `cancelled` from draft, approved or sent.
- **Reorder alerts:** a daily job and every issue compare free stock plus open purchase quantities with each rule; below the level, `procurement.reorder.draft` (platform-only) drafts one purchase order per preferred vendor and company with quantities and no rate, and tells the Inventory Manager. The Inventory Manager's edit fills each line's rate from the vendor's valid quote under their own grant, or by hand with `procurement.rate.read`.
- **Commands:** `procurement.po.create`, `.update` (a draft), `.approve` (question 5 (a); the limit on the grand total including GST), `.send` (the PDF to the vendor by email through the mailer, or marked sent by hand), `.cancel` (a reason), `.close` (short-closed with a reason); `procurement.grn.post` (lines, bins and every serial of a tracked item: scanned or typed, each matching the item's pattern, unique for the item, counted against the quantity; INV-04's second criterion); `procurement.grn.reverse` (the whole receipt, while none of its stock or serials has moved on). Posting moves stock in through `app.stock_move()` (`receipt`), revalues the moving average (question 2 (a)), raises `procurement.receipt.posted` and fills backorders through H2's worker.
- **The scan field** (`apps/web/src/screens/scan`, question 8 (a)): one input that takes a handheld scanner's keystrokes or the camera's reading, says at once whether the code is known, a repeat or the wrong pattern, and keeps the count; used by U2's receipt, then D2's picking and H3's counts.
- **Documents:** the purchase order PDF on the company's letterhead (rates and the tax split, light, A4), a template in the print registry.
- **Screens:** `/procurement/orders` (list by state, the reorder alerts on top), the order sheet, Receive goods (by purchase order, line by line, serials by scan), `/procurement/receipts`; reorder rules on the item's stock card.
- **Acceptance:** INV-04's criteria; a receipt of a serial-tracked item with one serial missing is refused; the purchase order's values are absent for a General Manager and the Store Manager on every screen and route; the PDF prints rates only for a holder of `procurement.rate.read`.

### 7.3 H3 Stock counts
- **Tables:** `stock_counts` (`entity_id`, `warehouse_id`, `scope` (whole hub, chosen bins, or chosen items), `state`, `started_at`, `started_by`, `posted_at`, `posted_by`, `cancel_reason`); `stock_count_lines` (`count_id`, `bin_id`, `item_id`, `serial_id`, `expected_qty` (frozen at start), `counted_qty`, `counted_by`, `counted_at`, `recount_qty`). New machine `stock-count`: `open` → `counting` → `review` → `posted`, or `cancelled`.
- **Blind counting:** a counter (`inventory.stock.move`) sees bins and items, never the expected quantity; serials are counted by scan. Movements in a counted bin after the count started are listed on the review and folded into the expected quantity, so the count does not freeze the hub.
- **Review and posting:** the Inventory Manager (`inventory.stock.adjust`) sees each variance with its value (value only with `finance.cost.read`), asks for a recount of any line, and posts: each variance becomes a `count` movement, a serial found becomes `in_stock` and a serial missing becomes a loss with its reason; the count's sheet prints for the record.
- **Acceptance:** a posted count leaves the ledger equal to what was counted; a counter never sees the expected quantity in any screen or route; the exit gate's audit runs on this tool (§11).

### 7.4 Q1 Labels
- **Templates:** `label-template.ts` moves into the print registry with three kinds: a serial label (item, SKU, serial, company), a bin label (hub, bin code), and a package label (dispatch number, package n of m, customer and place, hub) for D2; sizes per question 9 (a); QR payloads of the form `SP1:S:<serial id>`, `SP1:B:<bin id>`, `SP1:P:<package id>`, parsed by `parseLabelPayload()` in `packages/domain`.
- **Printing:** Print labels on a receipt (every serial received), a bin, a serial and a hub's bins; reprint audited; a batch of 500 labels renders inside the print budget.
- **Scan back:** a BOS label scanned into the scan field opens or selects its serial, bin or package; an unknown or foreign code says so.
- **Acceptance:** INV-07: every label's QR decodes back to its payload with `jsqr` in the test, and on the client's phones, scanners, printer and stock in the final phase.

### 7.5 R3 Warranty claims and supplier returns
- **Table `warranty_claims`:** `entity_id`, `claim_no`, `account_id`, `site_id`, `serial_id`, `reported_fault`, `state` (the `warranty-claim` machine, now in use), `replacement_serial_id`, `vendor_id`, `supplier_rma_ref`, `settlement` (`credit` or `replacement`), restricted `replacement_cost` and `supplier_credit_amount` (question 10 (a)), `rejected_reason`; a new series kind `warranty_claim` (code `WC`).
- **Commands:** one per machine event: `inventory.warranty.raise` (on a customer, or a serial found by scan or search), `.identify_serial` (in warranty by `warranty_until`, IST), `.replace` (a replacement serial issued from stock through `app.stock_move()` as `warranty_replacement`; the failed serial moves to quarantine as `faulty` when it comes back), `.rma_raise` (the vendor and their reference; the failed serial to `with_supplier`), `.settle` (a supplier replacement received into stock, or a credit recorded), `.reject` (a reason). Each audited.

- **E-way bill watch:** an hourly job (`inventory.eway.watch`, platform-only) tells the dispatch's maker and the Inventory Manager when a dispatch in transit has an e-way bill lapsing within 6 hours (named default) or lapsed (INV-05's alert), and counts it for the Sentry alerts G3 adds.

- **Commands:** `inventory.dispatch.create`, `.pick`, `.ready`, `.eway_record`, `.depart`, `.deliver`, `.cancel`, each a machine event, each audited.
- **Screens:** `/inventory/dispatch` (by state: to pick, ready, in transit, delivered today; lapsing e-way bills on top), the dispatch sheet, the order page's Dispatch tab, and the picking screen built for a phone held in one hand.
- **Acceptance:** NFR-07 and INV-05: a dispatch above the threshold cannot depart without a valid e-way bill, by any path, including a direct call to the command; a split order across two hubs reaches `dispatched` only when every line is delivered; serials picked match the dispatch; the challan's number has no gaps.

## 9. Wave 4

### 9.1 R2 Returns and the surplus pool

## 14. Gaps and conflicts in the governing documents
- **Locations:** DATABASE §6.5 gives `warehouses` a `location_id` into `org_locations`, which does not exist; H1 keeps the hub's address on `warehouses`.
- **Stock availability for callers:** PRD SAL-04 shows availability on quotes, which Lead Converters build, but SECURITY §3.2 gives them no `inventory.stock.read` (question 4).
- **Purchase order approval:** BLUEPRINT §8.4 asks a person to approve a drafted purchase order, and SECURITY §3.2 has no approval permission (question 5).
- **Arrival:** the dispatch machine needs a photo on `deliver` and asks who records it; field engineers hold no dispatch permission and the app is Phase 4 (question 7).
- **Warranty cost:** the `warranty-claim` machine writes a `job_cost_entries` row, a Phase 5 table (question 10); `settle` is given to `procurement.grn.write` and `rma.raise` to `procurement.po.write`, so the Inventory Manager alone drives a claim end to end, which matches SECURITY §3.2.
