# Client packs and the Phase 0 gate

The documents the Shakti group and the development team work through together. The client packs are written for non-technical readers in business words; the gate checklist is the owner's and the developer's own. A living pack is kept current and carries a "last updated" date; a record is filled once and then kept.

| Pack | Purpose | Reader | Living or record |
|---|---|---|---|
| [progress-for-client.md](progress-for-client.md) | One page on what is built, what comes next and what the group must do | The group's owners | Living, replaced as the work moves on |
| [client-actions.md](client-actions.md) | Every item only the group can give, with who, the piece of work that waits for it, its state, and the sign-off record | The group's owners and the people they name | Living |
| [workshop-pack.md](workshop-pack.md) | The decisions already taken and every open question for the discovery workshop, with the answer record | The owners, sales head, Accounts, the CA and the engineering head | Living until the answers are recorded, then a record |
| [design-signoff.md](design-signoff.md) | The checklist and decision for the design system's sign-off | An Executive, with a tele-caller and a field or store person | Record once signed |
| [wireframe-review.md](wireframe-review.md) | The screen review with one or two real users per role: preparation, tasks, notes form and record | The development team running the sessions; the owner who picks the reviewers | Record once the sessions are held |
| [prototype/index.html](prototype/index.html) | The clickable prototype of the Phase 1 screens not yet built, one file that works offline; shared with reviewers by a private link | Reviewers in the screen review | Living until the screens it shows are built |
| [vendor-quotes.md](vendor-quotes.md) | One quote request per vendor, the letter template, the cost sheet and tracking | The owner, who approves and sends | Living until the quotes are in, then a record |
| [exit-gate-actions.md](exit-gate-actions.md) | The Phase 0 exit gate item by item, and every action by who acts next | The owner and the developer | Living until the deferred gate items close |
