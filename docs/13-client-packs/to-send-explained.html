Your checklist, explained

Thank you for the answers you have already given. Ten items on the checklist are still marked To send. This guide explains each one in simple words: what it is, why we need it, and exactly what to send.

Three things to know before you start
  1. Five items are needed now (05, 06, 07, 08, 10). Five are for later stages (12, 14, 15, 16, 18). There is no rush on those, and we will remind you before each one is needed.
  2. Send things as they are. A photo, an old Excel sheet or a rough list is fine. You do not need to clean or retype anything.
  3. Never send passwords to any website or portal. Where we need access, we ask you to add us instead.
How to send
  • Email to r4rupak1997@gmail.com, one email per item, with the item number in the subject, for example Shakti Prime – Item 05 – Letterhead, logo and bank details. The Email this button on each item fills this in for you.
  • After you send it, open the item on the checklist and mark it Sent. We mark it Received once we have checked it.
  • If anything is still unclear, call us and we will go through it together.

Needed now

These five help us finish the first stage: calling, leads, quotes, orders and dealer credit.

05Letterhead, logo and bank detailsAccounts
What it is
When the system prints a quote for a customer, it looks like your own company paper: your logo at the top, and your bank details at the bottom so the customer knows where to pay.
Why
Each of the four companies (Shakti Supreme, Shakti Motor Pumps, Agro Solar Hub, RCREF) needs its own look and its own bank account on its quotes. Without these, a quote cannot be printed.
What to send
For each company:
  • Logo: the logo image file your designer or printer gave you (PNG or JPG). If you do not have the file, a clear photo of the logo on white paper.
  • Letterhead: a blank letterhead as a PDF or Word file, or a clear photo of a printed one.
  • Bank details: a photo of a cancelled cheque of the account customers should pay into, and the UPI ID.
Good to know
If one company has no letterhead or logo yet, just tell us which one.
06Look at the screens and approve themOwner
What it is
We have built the screens your team will use every day. We want a few of your people to see them and tell us honestly if anything is hard to understand.
Why
It is much easier to change a screen now than after your whole team is using it.
What to do
  1. Choose three people: one owner or manager, one caller, and one person from the store or field team.
  2. Email us their names and one or two times that suit them, about 40 minutes each.
  3. We show the screens on a video call or during a visit. They try a few simple tasks and tell us what is confusing.
  4. Afterwards, the owner tells us "OK" or what to change.
Good to know
Nothing to prepare, and nothing to learn first. There are no wrong answers: if something is confusing, that is exactly what we want to hear.
07Your current customer and lead dataSales head
What it is
The customer and enquiry lists your team uses today, in Excel, Google Sheets or another CRM app.
Why
We move these lists into the new system, so your callers see every old customer and enquiry from day one and nobody starts from zero.
What to send
  • First, a small sample: a few hundred rows from your main sheet, exactly as it is today: names, phone numbers, villages, what they asked about, notes.
  • A short list of the sheets you use, for example "Solar enquiries 2026, kept by the calling team".
  • Later, the full files, when we are ready to move everything across.
Good to know
Do not clean, sort or retype anything. Different column names in different sheets are fine: we match them ourselves. If you use a CRM app, its "Export to Excel" or "Download" button gives the file.
08Dealer credit limitsAccounts
What it is
For dealers who buy on credit: the most money each dealer may owe you at one time (the credit limit), and how many days they get to pay a bill (the credit days).
Why
Before a dealer's new order is confirmed, the system checks two things: is the dealer within their limit, and is any of their bills overdue? If the dealer is over the limit or a bill is overdue, the order waits until an owner allows it. This protects you from unpaid money growing too large.
What to send
One Excel sheet with four columns, one row per dealer and company:

Also tell us the name of the person who will update how much each dealer owes, until the system reads it from Tally.
Good to know
If you have not decided a limit for some dealers yet, tell us that. Their orders will simply wait for an owner's OK each time until a limit is set.
10Customer consent and privacy wordingOwner
What it is
Three short pieces of text that customers see or hear:
  1. Privacy notice: a short page saying what customer details you keep (name, phone, address, bill) and why.
  2. Consent question: the sentence a caller says to ask permission, for example "May we call you and send you WhatsApp messages about solar and pumps?"
  3. Call recording notice: for example "This call may be recorded for quality and training."
Why
Indian law on personal data and on telemarketing calls requires that customers agree before you call or message them, and that they are told how their details are used. The system records who agreed, and when, so you can prove it if ever asked.
What to do
  1. You do not need to write anything. We send you draft wording.
  2. Read it. If you have a lawyer or your CA, show it to them.
  3. Reply "approved", or tell us what to change.

For later stages, no rush

These are listed now so you know what is coming. We will remind you before each one is needed.

12Voice samplesManagers
What it is
Later, managers will be able to ask the system a question by speaking, like talking to an assistant on the phone. Before we build it, we test that it understands how your team speaks.
What to send
20 to 30 short voice recordings, each 5 to 10 seconds, of managers asking the kind of question they would ask. Mix Hindi, Hinglish and English, for example:
"Aaj kitne naye leads aaye?" · "Show me today's pending quotes" · "Jaipur store mein 5 HP pump kitne hain?"
How to send
  • Record them as normal WhatsApp voice notes (for example, send them to yourself).
  • In WhatsApp, long-press a voice note, tap Share, choose Gmail or your email app, and send it to our address. You can select several notes at once.
  • Or put them all in one Google Drive folder and email us the link.
14Opening stock and a label printerStores, and your printer supplier
What it is
Opening stock means how many of each item are on your shelves on the day the system starts tracking stock. Labels are small stickers with a code that the system prints for each item, so it can be scanned with a phone.
Why
The stock in the system must match what is really on the shelves from the first day. Labels make counting and dispatch quick and accurate.
What to send
  • Stock count: one Excel sheet per store, with item name, quantity and store name. We tell you which day to count, nearer the time.
  • Label printer: the make and model of the label printer you have or plan to buy, and the label size. Your printer supplier can tell you, or send a photo of the sticker on the back of the printer.
15Project and subsidy papers, and document photosProject manager
What it is
For solar projects, many papers are filled for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and for the electricity company (DISCOM) approvals. The system will keep each project's papers in one place and prepare these forms for you.
What to send
  • One example of each form you fill today: for example the subsidy application, the DISCOM or net-metering application, the customer agreement and the completion report. Blank or filled, as scans or clear photos.
  • A few real customer document photos (an Aadhaar card, an electricity bill), only with the customer's permission. The system hides the Aadhaar number automatically, and we test that on real photos.
Good to know
Examples of the forms are enough. Please do not send us the login or password of the PM Surya Ghar portal or any other website.
16Tally accessAccounts
What it is
Your accounts are kept in Tally. Later, the system will read figures from Tally, such as balances and bills, to show reports. It never changes anything in Tally.
What to do
  1. Your Accounts person chooses a day for us to visit the computer that runs Tally, for about two hours.
  2. On that day, they give us a copy (backup) of one company's Tally data, which we use only for testing.
  3. They also give us your list of ledger heads. Tally can print or export it.
18What the AI assistants may do alone, and the final go-aheadOwner
What it is
The system has small automatic helpers, called AI assistants. For example, one sorts new leads by how likely they are to buy, and one helps callers with what to say next. For each helper, you decide how much it may do by itself:
  1. Only suggest: it shows an idea, and a person decides.
  2. Needs approval: it prepares the work, and a person must press OK first.
  3. Acts alone: for small, safe jobs only.
Separately, nothing goes live without your written OK at the end of each stage.
What to do
Nothing now. Before each stage goes live, we hold a short meeting, explain each helper in simple words, and you choose.
