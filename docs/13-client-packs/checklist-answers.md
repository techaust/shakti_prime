## 2. The answers

### Item 01 · Your working rules
**Lead journey (Shakti Supreme, solar).**
1. **Cold calling.** Cold callers make high-volume outbound calls to generate enquiries. Interested leads are entered in the CRM and assigned to a lead converter (LC).
2. **Lead converter, first contact.** The LC contacts the customer and collects: customer name, mobile number, full address, electricity bill or monthly consumption, electricity connection details, the requirement and desired solar capacity, a manual location and a live GPS location. The aim is to book a site visit.
3. **Site visit.** The technical or site-survey team receives the lead with everything recorded so far, filled in automatically with no second entry: name, mobile number and address stay the same, and the bill and location are at hand. The survey adds the roof dimensions and available area, roof type and condition, shadow analysis, installation feasibility, the proposed system capacity, site photographs and technical remarks.
4. **Quotation.** Prepared from the survey's findings, with pricing and technical details checked, assigned to the salesperson concerned, shared with the customer, and its sending by WhatsApp or email recorded in the CRM.
5. **Follow-up and negotiation.** Reminders made automatically; the customer's feedback, price objections and negotiation recorded; every interaction given a next action; interested customers moved towards order confirmation.
6. **Order.** Customer approval, advance payment, order confirmation, material procurement, installation and commissioning, final payment and closure.

**What callers record:** customer name, address, required load, monthly electricity bill and the customer's response.

**Unanswered leads (CALL-3):** "there are three probabilities so day 1 then gap of 2 days day 2 gap of 2 days and final 3rd day with Telecaller cheat code with a final conclusion".

**How long a lead stays with one LC (CALL-4):** 7 days, with 6 to 7 attempts in all.

| Days | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | First call and introduction |
| 2 to 3 | 2 to 3 follow-ups |
| 4 to 5 | Further follow-ups and a site-visit attempt |
| 6 to 7 | Final follow-ups (6 to 7 attempts in all) |
| After day 7 | Review: keep, nurture or reassign |

**Quote numbers (SALE-1):** separate automatic numbering for each business.

### Item 03 · How GST is worked out (the group's answer; the CA has not yet confirmed it)
1. **Which state's tax applies (PRICE-5):** the group is registered in Rajasthan; within Rajasthan CGST and SGST apply, and between states IGST, depending on the place of supply.
2. **Rounding (PRICE-5):** GST is calculated on the taxable value at the applicable rate, and the final tax amount is rounded to the nearest rupee, by the same method as the group's accounting and invoicing software.
3. **Solar 70:30 (PRICE-6):** for solar EPC projects, 70 % goods and materials (panels, inverter, structure, cables and the rest) and 30 % installation, commissioning and related services. Of the items, solar panels and solar inverters are at 5 % and everything else at 18 %. The split is subject to the GST classification and treatment of each contract.
4. **Past invoices:** the group can send 5 to 10 past GST invoices with customer names and confidential details blacked out.
5. **The CA:** the group can arrange a short call with its CA to confirm the GST rates, HSN and SAC codes, the state-wise treatment, the rounding method and the EPC billing structure.

### Item 04 · Engineering values
**Electric pumps — what is asked of the customer:** the horsepower wanted; the water level of the area, and which area; a 4-inch or 6-inch submersible; single or three phase; submersible, openwell or monoblock.

**Solar pumps:** the company chart, to be shared separately.

**Rooftop structures:**

| System | Panels | Legs | Rafters | Channels | Height | Area |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 kW | 5 to 6 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 6 to 8 ft | 13 × 14 ft (182 sq ft) |
| 5 kW | 9 to 10 | 6 | 3 | 4 | 6 to 8 ft | 14 × 20 ft (280 sq ft) |
| 6 kW | 11 to 12 | 8 | 4 | 4 | 6 to 8 ft | 24 × 14 ft (336 sq ft) |
| 8 kW, double string (more stable) | 15 to 16 | 10 | 5 | 4 | 10 × 8 ft or 11 × 9 ft | 32 × 14 ft (448 sq ft) |
| 8 kW, triple string (space optimised) | 15 to 16 | 9 | 3 | 6 | 10 × 8 ft or 11 × 9 ft | 21 × 20 ft (420 sq ft) |
| 10 kW, double string (best strength) | 18 to 20 | 12 | 6 | 8 | 10 × 8 ft or 11 × 9 ft | 30 × 14 ft (420 sq ft) |
| 10 kW, triple string (better space use) | 18 to 20 | 12 | 6 | 8 | 10 × 8 ft or 11 × 9 ft | 24 × 21 ft (504 sq ft) |

**Practical notes:**
- More kW means more rafters and legs, for load distribution. Double string is stronger and wind-safe; triple string uses space better but needs proper bracing.
- Height: small systems 6 to 8 ft; large systems 10 to 11 ft at the front and 8 to 9 ft at the back slope, for cleaning access, airflow and a cooler panel, which generates better.
- Area: 1 kW needs 80 to 100 sq ft on an elevated structure.
- Which to use: a high-wind area, double string; a small terrace, triple string; a premium client, double string; a cost-sensitive client, triple string.

### Item 09 · Targets for callers and teams
**Working time:** 9:30 AM to 5:30 or 6:00 PM, with 30 minutes for lunch and 30 minutes for tea and personal breaks, about 7 productive hours a day, 26 working days a month. The aim is to measure calls, qualified leads, site visits, orders and the solar capacity booked.

**Cold caller.** Generates the most enquiries and finds potential solar customers. Initial calls last 1 to 2 minutes.

| Measure | Daily | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dial attempts | 200 to 250 | 1,200 to 1,500 | 5,200 to 6,500 |
| Connected calls | 60 to 80 | 360 to 480 | 1,560 to 2,080 |
| Interested customers | 20 to 25 | 120 to 150 | 520 to 650 |
| Qualified leads | 10 | 60 | 260 |
| CRM entries | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |

A qualified lead has: the customer's name and number; location and property details; the electricity bill or consumption; rooftop availability or the possibility of installation; genuine interest in solar; permission to follow up.

**Lead converter.** Turns qualified enquiries into serious prospects and site visits; at most 6 to 7 follow-ups per lead, depending on the customer's response and interest.

| Measure | Daily | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh leads handled | 8 to 10 | 48 to 60 | 208 to 260 |
| Follow-up attempts | 20 to 25 | 120 to 150 | 520 to 650 |
| Meaningful conversations | 10 to 15 | 60 to 90 | 260 to 390 |
| Site visits scheduled | 2 to 3 | 12 to 18 | 52 to 78 |
| Site visits confirmed | 1 to 2 | 6 to 12 | 26 to 52 |
| CRM updates | 100 % | 100 % | 100 % |

**Sales executive or site-visit team.** Site assessment, technical feasibility, quotations and closing the order. An order counts only once the customer has formally confirmed the project under the group's booking policy.

| Measure | Daily | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site visits | 2 | 12 | 52 |
| Technical assessments | 2 | 12 | 52 |
| Quotations | 2 | 12 | 52 |
| Orders closed | Tracked | 1 to 2 | 5 to 8 |
| Capacity booked | Tracked | 5 to 10 kW | 25 to 40 kW |

**A sales unit** (one cold caller, one lead converter and one sales executive), each month: 260 qualified leads, 26 to 40 completed site visits at first, 5 to 8 orders and 25 to 40 kW booked. These are initial planning targets, to be set again after 30 days of real conversion data.

**Rating against target:** below 80 % underperforming; 80 to 99 % needs improvement; 100 to 119 % target achieved; 120 % or above excellent. Each person is judged on their own responsibilities: a cold caller on qualified leads, not orders.

**Daily report,** submitted by every employee before leaving (with the example given): calls attempted (220), calls connected (70), interested customers (22), qualified leads (10), leads passed to an LC (10), follow-ups done (24), site visits scheduled (3), site visits completed (2), quotations issued (2), orders booked (1), kW booked (5).

**Management rules:**
1. Every enquiry is entered in the CRM.
2. No lead is passed on without basic qualification.
3. Every lead has an assigned owner.
4. Every follow-up has a date and a purpose.
5. Hot leads get priority.
6. Site visits are tracked separately as scheduled, confirmed and completed.
7. Booked kW, installed kW and collected revenue are reported separately.
8. Daily performance is reviewed at 6 PM.

**The five benchmarks to start with:** cold caller 200 calls a day; 10 qualified leads a day; lead converter 2 site visits a day; sales executive 2 site visits a day; sales unit 25 to 40 kW a month.
