# User acceptance: Sales Team Lead

The Sales Team Lead looks after one team of callers: their queues and targets, and the leads the team works.

**Before you start:** sign in as a person with the Sales Team Lead role in one company, on the address the development team gives you (staging until production exists). Use the test customers and leads prepared for the test, never a real customer's details. Tick Pass when the result is what the third column says; tick Fail and write what you saw when it is not. A step that fails does not stop the test: carry on, and give the sheet to the run owner at the end.

| # | Step | Expected result | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open **Home**. | The team's targets with live meters, a leaderboard of the team's callers, and the team's queues. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 2 | On the leaderboard, switch between calls, qualified, orders and kW, and between day, week and month. | The ranking changes with each choice. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 3 | Open **Targets** and set a daily call target for one caller of your team. | The target is listed among the targets in force; the caller's home shows it. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 4 | Open **Leads**. | Your team's leads; no lead of another team or company appears. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 5 | Open one lead of a caller of your team and assign it to another caller of your team. | The lead shows its new owner; the timeline records the change. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 6 | Open **Calling**. | Your own queue, and the team's queues you may look into. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 7 | Open **Converting** and choose My team, then one person. | That person's leads on the converting board. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 8 | Open **Lead converters**. | The people of your team who take qualified leads, with their presence and the most leads each takes. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
