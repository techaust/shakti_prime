# User acceptance: Store Manager

The Store Manager serves walk-in customers at the store: a lead, a quote and an order, on the same screens as a Lead Converter.

**Before you start:** sign in as a person with the Store Manager role in one company, on the address the development team gives you (staging until production exists). Use the test customers and leads prepared for the test, never a real customer's details. Tick Pass when the result is what the third column says; tick Fail and write what you saw when it is not. A step that fails does not stop the test: carry on, and give the sheet to the run owner at the end.

| # | Step | Expected result | Pass / Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open **Walk-in customer** and enter a customer who came to the store, with their consent to be called. | The lead is saved as yours, with the consent recorded. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 2 | Open **Customers** and open that customer. | Their page shows the lead, the site, the consent and the timeline. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 3 | On the customer's page, add a note. | The note appears on the timeline with your name. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 4 | Open **Converting** and open the walk-in lead. | The lead opens with its calls, sizing and quote parts. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 5 | Record the sizing and build a quote. | A quote number is given at the customer's price tier, with tax worked out. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 6 | Open the quote and choose **Mark as sent**, then **Record acceptance** with the signed copy. | The quote is accepted and its order is made. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 7 | Open **Orders** and confirm the order. | The order is confirmed; the lead is won. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 8 | Open **Leads**. | Only your own leads. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
| 9 | Try to open **Dealer credit**. | There is no Dealer credit item in your menu. | ☐ Pass ☐ Fail |
