# Shakti Prime: where the work stands

Prepared 04-10-2026 · last updated 07-10-2026. For: the owners of the Shakti group. Prepared by the development team. One page; the development team replaces it as the work moves on.

## What is built
The system runs on two private sites, one for building and one for review, with invented data only. Nothing is live for daily work yet. Everything below works on the review site today.

**Leads and calling**
- **Leads:** the leads list with search, the New lead form, the Walk-in customer form for someone at the counter, and the lead board where a lead moves from stage to stage, is handed to a colleague, is set aside for later follow-up, or is marked lost. A lead is won once sales orders exist.
- **Calling:** the screen for cold callers. It shows the day's queue (callbacks that are due first, then priority and age), records how each call went with one key, and sets the next step itself: a callback, up to three tries for an unanswered lead, then follow-up calls on day 7, 30 and 90. A call is refused outside calling hours or without the customer's consent. A team lead sees the team's queues.
- **Pipelines and priority:** each sales pipeline's stages, the call outcomes and the rules that rank a lead, set by an Executive; referral partners get a code that credits their leads to them.
- **Duplicates:** the system spots the same customer entered twice and the same enquiry coming again. A person decides to merge or keep apart, and a merge can be undone. A repeat enquiry joins the lead that is already open.

**Customers, quotes and prices**
- **Customers:** one record per customer for the whole group, with each company's own relationship; the customer's page shows contacts, sites, leads, the history of the customer's leads and notes, tasks, tags and consent with its proof.
- **Quotes:** a quote is made from the customer's price list, with GST worked out by the system, numbered without gaps for each company and year, then sent, withdrawn or quoted again. A quote is valid for 15 days and expires by itself after that.
- **Printed quotes:** each company's letterhead, logo and bank account, printed in the light style whatever theme the person uses.
- **Sizing:** the pump and rooftop sizing rules, which the quotes use. They work on common engineering values until the group's engineering head confirms the real ones.
- **Products and prices:** the catalogue of items and kits with their specifications and pump curves, the price lists for each tier with kit prices and the full history of every price change, and the GST rate screen for Accounts.
- **Imports:** a spreadsheet of leads or customers uploaded, its columns matched, every row checked, then added in one go; the PIN code list is loaded the same way.

**People and control**
- **Signing in safely:** passwords with a lockout after repeated mistakes, and an authenticator app for Executives, the General Manager and Accounts.
- **Team members and roles:** people invited with a role in each company, each role's permissions changed by an Executive, and the Activity log of who changed what and when.
- **AI assistants:** the Agent Inbox, where an assistant's suggestions wait for a person to approve them, and the Agents screen, where an Executive stops an assistant or sets its daily spending limit. No assistant works yet: the AI service accounts are still to be connected.
- **Search:** one search box that finds a lead by phone, name or village, including names spelled differently, and a quote by its number.
- **Looks:** light and dark themes, a higher-contrast option for phones in sunlight, and screens that work on a phone.

## What is being built now

2. Sales orders with the dealer credit check, including releasing an order held for credit.
3. The Knowledge Vault, where approved company documents are kept and searched.
