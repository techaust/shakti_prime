# Shakti Prime: where the work stands

Prepared 04-10-2026 · last updated 04-10-2026. For: the owners of the Shakti group. Prepared by the development team. One page; the development team replaces it as the work moves on.

## What is built
The system runs on two private sites, one for building and one for review, with invented data only. Nothing is live for daily work yet.

**Working on the review site today:**
- **Signing in safely:** passwords with a lockout after repeated mistakes, and an authenticator app for Executives, the General Manager and Accounts.
- **Leads:** the leads list with search, the New lead form, and the lead board where a lead moves from stage to stage, is handed to a colleague, is set aside for later follow-up, or is marked lost (a lead is won once quotes and orders exist).
- **Customers:** one record per customer for the whole group, with each company's own relationship; the customer's page shows contacts, sites, leads, the history of the customer's leads and notes, tasks, tags and consent with its proof.
- **Products and prices:** the catalogue of items and kits with their specifications and pump curves, the price lists for each tier with kit prices and the full history of every price change, and the GST rate screen for Accounts.
- **Imports:** a spreadsheet of leads uploaded, its columns matched, every row checked, then added in one go.
- **People and control:** team members invited with a role in each company, each role's permissions changed by an Executive, and the Activity log of who changed what and when.
- **Search:** one search box that finds a lead by phone, name or village, including names spelled differently.
- **Looks:** light and dark themes, a higher-contrast option for phones in sunlight, and screens that work on a phone.

**Built and waiting to be added:** printed quotes on each company's letterhead, the sales pipeline settings with lead priority and referral codes, and the pump and rooftop sizing calculators.
