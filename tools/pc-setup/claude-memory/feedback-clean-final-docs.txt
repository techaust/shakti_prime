When delivering a final plan or document, write it as a clean, fresh, definitive version. Leave out change-log language such as "revised", "dropped", "added", "trimmed", "replaces X", "(new)", version notes, or keep/drop lists.

**Why:** the user said the final plan "should look like fresh and rock solid" after spotting leftover revision wording.
**How to apply:** express scope as a neutral "in scope / outside scope" statement, and keep revision history out of the deliverable. Grep for revision words before handing it over. Related: [[shakti-prime-blueprint]].
