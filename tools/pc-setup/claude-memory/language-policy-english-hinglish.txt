On 2026-09-27 the user decided (via interview) to drop Hindi from the whole product and plan:
- All screens, buttons, errors, in-app help tips, WhatsApp and SMS messages (templates and the Concierge agent's replies), emails and customer documents (quotes, proformas, challans, handover kit PDFs) are **English only**.
- **Hinglish, Roman script only** for: tele-caller scripts and co-pilot lines, voice agent speech (customer calls and Talk to Shakti), training videos and live sessions. The speech engine may convert Roman Hinglish to Devanagari internally for pronunciation; that text is never shown or stored.
- `contacts.preferred_language` becomes `'hinglish'` (default) or `'en'` and controls **calls only**: the voice agent's language and which caller-script variant a caller sees.
- Database: a new forward-only migration drops every `name_hi` (roles, lead sources, pipelines, stages, price tiers, items, kits, contacts, accounts) and `users.locale`, and changes the preferred_language check. Earlier migrations stay untouched. Nothing is hosted yet.
- Documents: BLUEPRINT, PRD, docs/08-design-system.md, AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, ARCHITECTURE, DATABASE, ROADMAP, KICKOFF-PROMPT, the week 3-5 design doc all updated to read as the definitive policy.
- Timing: after AUDIT.md is delivered, **before** the design batch ([[design-profile-linear]]), so the design batch loads Inter only (no Noto Sans Devanagari).

Engineering choices stated to the user (not asked): keep next-intl with a single English catalogue; copy lint drops the en/hi parity rule and adds a rule rejecting Devanagari in catalogues and templates; transliteration-aware search and `search_roman` leave the plan (pg_trgm fuzzy search stays); the week 6 "Chromium Devanagari PDF" spike becomes a PDF and label rendering spike; the speech benchmark adds Roman-Hinglish pronunciation as a criterion.

**Why:** the user wants one interface language; Hinglish only where staff or the AI speak to customers.
**How to apply:** approved docs are hand-edited in targeted lines and must read fresh, no change-log wording ([[feedback-clean-final-docs]]).
